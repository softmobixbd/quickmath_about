Welcome to QuickMath Quest!

At SoftMobiXBD, we believe that learning should be both fun and engaging. With the increasing use of mobile devices, we wanted to create something educational and entertaining—a game that transforms screen time into a productive learning experience.

Why We Created QuickMath Quest

Many people spend hours on mobile devices, often engaging with content that doesn’t contribute to their growth. That’s why we developed QuickMath Quest—a fun and interactive way to sharpen math skills while enjoying the challenge of a game. Whether you love numbers or want to improve your math-solving skills, this app provides an enjoyable way to practice addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

Who Can Play?

QuickMath Quest is perfect for students, kids, and adults who want a fun daily brain workout. Levels start easy and ramp up gently, so it works whether you're a student practicing for exams or an adult keeping your mind sharp.

What Makes Our App Special?

Unlike many other math games, QuickMath Quest offers fully randomized questions, ensuring that each play-through is unique. The app now includes 12 game modes:

  • ✅ Simple Math – Solve problems at your own pace
  • ✅ Algebraic Math – Multi-step equations that build real fluency
  • ✅ Find X, Y, Z – Solve algebra equations and master variables
  • ✅ Time Challenge – Answer as many questions as possible in a set time
  • ✅ Time Limit – Score as high as you can before time runs out
  • ✅ True or False – Quickly decide if the math statement is correct
  • ✅ Drag & Match – Move the correct answer to the question
  • ✅ Draw to Match – Connect the right answer with a line
  • ✅ Sudoku – Classic logic puzzles from beginner to hard
  • ✅ 2048 – Slide and merge number tiles in this addictive puzzle
  • ✅ 2248 – Connect matching number tiles in a chain and merge them into bigger numbers
  • ✅ Match 10 – Pair numbers that are equal or add up to 10 and clear the board

These diverse game styles make math practice more engaging and dynamic! Play once a day to build your streak, unlock achievement badges, and watch your accuracy climb — all your progress is saved right on your device.
