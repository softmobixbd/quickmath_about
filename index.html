Why we built it

Turning screen time into brain time.

At SoftMobiXBD we think learning should be genuinely fun — not homework with a play button on it.

Most of us lose hours a day to a screen, and very little of it leaves us sharper than we started. QuickMath Quest is our answer to that: a game you actually want to open, that happens to make you faster and more confident with numbers.

So we built the app we wanted to hand our own families. No account wall before the first puzzle. No progress held hostage by a server. No difficulty spike that makes a ten-year-old give up in level three. Just clean, fast, endlessly varied maths — from a two-second addition check all the way to multi-step algebra.

Play a single round a day and you build a streak. Keep the streak and you build a habit. That is the whole idea — small daily reps beat one long cram session.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Is QuickMath Quest free?
Yes. All 20 game modes are free to play, with no subscription and no in-app purchases. The app is supported by ads.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. Every game mode works fully offline. A connection is only used to load ads and to check for app updates — the maths never needs one.
Are there ads?
Yes, the app is ad-supported through Google AdMob, and ads are configured to stay family-friendly. Some modes also offer an optional rewarded ad if you want an extra life to continue a run — watching one is always your choice.
Is it safe for children?
Yes. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no chat, and we never collect names, emails or any other personal details. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture.
Where is my progress saved?
Your streak, stats, achievements and level progress are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to a server, which also means they do not transfer if you switch phones or uninstall the app.
What ages is it for?
Roughly six and up. Early levels only need basic addition and subtraction, while the algebra, sudoku and timed modes stay challenging for adults.
What languages does the app support?
QuickMath Quest is available in English, Bengali, Hindi, Indonesian, Portuguese and Spanish, and follows your device language automatically.
How do I report a bug or suggest a game mode?
Email us at softmobixbd@gmail.com. Player suggestions are where most of our new modes come from — 2248 and Match 10 both started that way.
